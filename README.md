## Projects

**[PharmaGuard](https://github.com/Abhishek-M-29/PharmaGuard)** &nbsp;`2026`&nbsp; — Pharmacogenomic Risk Engine

Analyzes patient VCF genomic data against CPIC clinical guidelines; generates LLM-powered drug risk reports with genotype-based classification and full clinical explainability. Deployed end-to-end on Azure.

`FastAPI` `PharmCAT` `Docker` `LLM` `Azure VM`

---

**[VaidyaBot](https://github.com/Abhishek-M-29/VaidyaBot)** &nbsp;`2025`&nbsp; — Voice Medical Assistant &nbsp;&nbsp; `🥇 1st · State Hackathon`

Multilingual voice-based medical assistant built on a custom RAG pipeline. Patients interact in regional languages and receive contextually accurate, retrieval-grounded health responses in real time.

`Flask` `MongoDB` `RAG` `Multilingual TTS/STT`

---

**[Explainable Diagnosis System](https://github.com/Abhishek-M-29/Explainable-Diagnosis-System)** &nbsp;`2025`&nbsp; — Diagnostic AI &nbsp;&nbsp; `🥈 2nd · National Hackathon`

Transparent diagnostic pipeline combining RAG + CAG to surface not just answers but *reasoning traces* — making AI-assisted diagnosis interpretable and trustworthy for clinical use.

`RAG` `CAG` `Explainable AI` `Healthcare NLP`
